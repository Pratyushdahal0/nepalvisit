Overview

The Langtang Valley trek is Nepal's most accessible high-altitude trek — just 80km north of Kathmandu, reachable by a single road journey with no flights required. Often called the "Valley of Glaciers," Langtang offers Himalayan drama (Langtang Lirung at 7,227m dominates the skyline), dense forests, glacial moraines, yak pastures and authentic Tamang culture.

Compared to EBC or the Annapurna Circuit, Langtang is quieter, cheaper and faster to access. It's the best-value major trek in Nepal — and one of the most underrated. Trekkers who do Langtang consistently say they expected it to be a consolation prize for not doing EBC, and end up completely captivated.

Post-earthquake recovery: The 2015 earthquake devastated Langtang village. The rebuilt villages along the trail are now fully operational, and trekking here directly supports local families who rebuilt their livelihoods in mountain tourism. Your visit matters here more than on more crowded routes.

Why Choose Langtang Over EBC?

  • No flights needed — drive from Kathmandu in 6–9 hours
  • Significantly cheaper — $500–$900 vs $1,400–$2,500 for EBC
  • Far fewer crowds — you'll often have the trail to yourself
  • Tamang culture is genuinely different from Sherpa culture on EBC
  • Wildlife: red panda (rare), langur monkeys, Himalayan tahr are all possible sightings
  • Can be done in 7 days — better for limited time

Permits & Fees 2026

Langtang National Park Entry Permit

NPR 3,000 ~$22 USD

Mandatory for all foreign trekkers. Checked at Dhunche checkpoint. Can be obtained at the NTB office in Kathmandu (Bhrikutimandap) or at Dhunche.

TIMS Card

NPR 2,000 ~$15 USD

Required for Langtang — unlike Annapurna where TIMS enforcement is mixed, Langtang checkpoints consistently require it. Get it in Kathmandu before you leave.

Total permits: ~NPR 5,000 (~$37–50 USD). A licensed guide through a registered agency is mandatory for foreign trekkers in Langtang in 2026. Solo trekking is not permitted.

Daily On-Trail Budget

  • Carry NPR 3,500–5,500 per day in cash for food, extras (WiFi, charging, hot showers) and tips
  • No ATMs after Syabrubesi — withdraw everything you need in Kathmandu or Trishuli
  • Dal bhat starts at NPR 500–600 ($4–5) at lower elevations, rises to NPR 800–1,000 ($6–8) at Kyanjin Gompa
  • WiFi in most teahouses: NPR 200–500 ($1.50–4) per day

Kyanjin Gompa — The Heart of the Trek

Kyanjin Gompa at 3,870m is the endpoint of most Langtang itineraries and easily the most rewarding destination on the route. The ancient Buddhist monastery, the surrounding glaciers, the yak cheese factory and the viewpoint hikes above make this place genuinely special.

The Yak Cheese Factory

Kyanjin is famous for its traditional yak cheese factory — one of the few remaining in Nepal. You can watch the cheese-making process and buy fresh yak cheese to eat on the trail. It's a small thing that sticks with you long after the trek ends.

Viewpoint Hikes from Kyanjin

  • Kyanjin Ri (4,773m): 3–4 hours return from Kyanjin Gompa. Panoramic views of the Langtang range. Easier than Tserko Ri. Good for one acclimatization day before going higher.
  • Tserko Ri (4,984m): 5–7 hours return. More challenging, more rewarding. 360-degree views of Langtang Lirung, Dorje Lakpa, Gangchenpo and more. Start early — summit before 10am for clearest views.
  • Yala Peak (5,500m): Requires 2 extra days and more technical preparation. Not a standard trekking peak — only for fit, experienced trekkers with proper gear.
Altitude at Kyanjin: You sleep at 3,870m. Take the approach days seriously — don't rush from Lama Hotel to Kyanjin in one day. The gradual ascent in the itinerary above is designed for proper acclimatization.

Practical Tips

  • The Syabrubesi drive: 6–9 hours from Kathmandu depending on road conditions. The road is rough and winding — take motion sickness medication if needed. Shared jeeps ($40–60) are faster and more comfortable than the local bus ($15).
  • Start early from Kathmandu: Leave by 7am to reach Syabrubesi before dark. There's nothing to do in Syabrubesi except sleep — it's purely a jumping-off point.
  • Langtang village memorial: The rebuilt Langtang village has a memorial to the 350+ people killed in the 2015 earthquake avalanche. It's a somber but important place to spend time.
  • Keep electronics warm: Cold temperatures above 3,500m drain batteries rapidly. Keep your phone inside your sleeping bag at night. Bring a power bank.
  • Wildlife sightings: Red pandas are theoretically possible in the forests between Syabrubesi and Lama Hotel, but sightings are extremely rare. Langur monkeys and Himalayan tahr (mountain goat) are more common. Keep your eyes open in the forest sections.
  • Gosaikunda extension: For experienced trekkers, the Gosaikunda Lake extension (4,380m, sacred Hindu and Buddhist lake) adds 2–3 days and is a spectacular addition. Requires crossing Laurebina La pass (4,610m).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Langtang Valley trek cost in 2026?

Standard guided packages cost $599–$900 per person for 8 days, including permits, guide, accommodation, meals and transport. Budget independent trekkers (with mandatory guide) can manage $450–$600. This makes Langtang significantly cheaper than EBC ($1,400–$2,500) because there are no flights involved.

Is Langtang better than EBC for first-time trekkers?

For first-time trekkers with limited time and budget, Langtang is genuinely excellent — moderate difficulty, no flights, lower cost, less crowded, and very beautiful. EBC has the iconic factor but costs nearly twice as much and requires more time. If you're wondering whether to do Langtang or EBC, do Langtang first — you might find you love it more.

How do I get to Syabrubesi from Kathmandu?

Local bus from Gongabu Bus Park (New Bus Park) in Kathmandu: 7–9 hours, NPR 500–700 ($4–5). Shared jeep: 6–7 hours, $40–60 per seat. Private jeep: $100–150. Most trekking agencies arrange jeep transport as part of the package.

What wildlife can I see on Langtang trek?

Langtang National Park is home to red pandas (very rare to spot), snow leopards (almost never seen), Himalayan tahr (mountain goats, commonly seen above 3,500m), langur monkeys (common in lower forests), and dozens of bird species including the Himalayan monal (Nepal's national bird). Wildlife sightings are more likely in spring and autumn.

Can I combine Langtang with Gosaikunda?

Yes — the Langtang-Gosaikunda combination is a classic 12–14 day route. From Kyanjin Gompa, retrace to Thulo Syabru, then ascend to Gosaikunda Lake via Laurebina La (4,610m), finishing at Dhunche or Sundarijal (Kathmandu). Requires strong fitness and extra permits.
